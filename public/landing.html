How is Prime different from just using ChatGPT?
▼
Prime is built specifically for students. While ChatGPT is general-purpose, Prime combines a study-focused chatbot with a Pomodoro timer, progress analytics, streak tracking, and a dedicated report writer — all in one place. It's not just chat, it's a complete study system designed around how you actually learn.
What subjects does Prime support?
▼
All of them. Prime covers every school and university subject — Mathematics, Sciences, History, Literature, Languages, Economics, Computer Science, and everything in between. If you can study it, Prime can help you with it.
Is my data private and secure?
▼
Absolutely. Your conversations, reports, and study data are encrypted and never sold to third parties. Your academic content belongs to you alone. We comply with GDPR, COPPA, and follow strict data privacy principles across every part of the platform.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
▼
Yes, completely. No lock-in contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel Basic or Pro from your account settings anytime and you'll retain full access until the end of your current billing period. No questions asked.
Does Prime work on mobile and tablet?
▼
Yes — Prime is fully responsive and works great on desktop, tablet, and mobile browser. A dedicated native app with offline support is on the roadmap, so you'll soon be able to study anywhere, with or without a connection.
