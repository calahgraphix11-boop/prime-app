How to delete your account

You can request deletion of your Prime account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do this:

  1. Open the Prime app, go to your Profile, tap Settings, and select "Delete Account." Confirm the action when prompted.
  2. Or send a deletion request by email or WhatsApp using the contact details below. Include the email address linked to your Prime account.

What data gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your profile (name, email, photo)
  • Study sessions, streaks, and analytics
  • StudyPal chat history
  • Exam Coach results and history
  • Note summaries
  • Study group memberships and messages you posted
  • Friends and leaderboard data

Data retention

Your personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days of your request. Anonymized usage statistics that cannot be linked back to you may be retained for analytics purposes.

Payment transaction records may be retained where required by law or for accounting purposes, but these are not linked to an active account after deletion.
